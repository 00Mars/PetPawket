About CHARM

About CHARM Foundation

CHARM stands for Caring Hearts for Animal Rescue and Medicine. We exist to keep rescue care moving with warmth, transparency, and deep respect for every pet story.

Our work honors families, fosters, and rescue partners without commodifying care.

Our values

Care in motion.

We lead with empathy, publish clear outcomes, and create lasting support for pets and the people who love them.

How we work

Step 1

Partner intake

Care funding

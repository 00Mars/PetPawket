Partner intake

Partner intake for rescue care.

Rescues, clinics, and community partners can request urgent support through CHARM Foundation.

We respond quickly to time-sensitive needs and keep communication warm, clear, and respectful.

How it works

Share the need, get the care.

Submit details about the request, and our team will follow up with next steps and available resources.

For emergencies, include a direct phone number in your message.

Partner intake form

We respond within 1-2 business days.

What we can support

Urgent medical care

Emergency procedures, diagnostics, and recovery supplies.

Rescue missions

Transport support, temporary housing, and intake coordination.

Placement support

Foster stipends, supplies, and adoption readiness.

Eligibility & required details

Eligibility checklist

  • Verified rescue, clinic, or community partner
  • Time-sensitive or urgent care need
  • Clear care plan and estimated timeline

Details to include

  • Urgency level and immediate needs
  • Partner contact and vet details
  • Estimated costs and expected outcomes

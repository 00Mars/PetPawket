Partner readiness checklist

What we ask you to share

  • Urgency level and care timeline.
  • Primary contact for approvals.
  • Current funding or resource gaps.
  • Permission for story and update sharing.

What CHARM provides

  • Designated fund tracking and receipts.
  • Care coordination with clinics.
  • Volunteer or transport support (as available).
  • Community updates with partner approval.

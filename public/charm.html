Privacy

CHARM should show care, not private records.

CHARM can carry hope, totals, milestones, and ready-to-share updates. It should not publish private family circumstances, raw veterinary details, private journals, memorial notes, or rescue material that is not ready.

Shared when ready: totals, summaries, updates. Protected: medical notes, hardship details, raw stories. Coming later: care support and planning tools.

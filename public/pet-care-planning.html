Care Planning Choose the kind of help you are looking for

These choices stay separate so families can understand what is earned, what is charitable, and what comes from licensed insurance partners.

Pawket Care Credit

Rewards you earn through Pet Pawket.

Pawket Care Credit is planned as earned rewards value from eligible Pet Pawket shopping and participation. It may later be usable in approved ways under program terms.

Pawket Care Credit is not insurance and does not provide guaranteed payment for any pet-care need.

CHARM Emergency Assistance

Charitable help for urgent animal care.

CHARM Emergency Assistance is planned as a charitable help path for families, rescue partners, and urgent animal-care needs that fit the mission.

Assistance is subject to eligibility, mission fit, available funds, and program guidelines.

Licensed Insurance Partners

A separate path for pet insurance.

For families who want actual pet insurance, Pet Pawket may connect customers to licensed insurance providers or agencies.

Pet Pawket does not underwrite, sell, administer, or guarantee insurance policies.

Clear Boundaries

Important details before this goes live.

These notes keep each kind of care help honest while Pet Pawket prepares the rules, reviews, and legal guidance needed before launch.

Pawket Care Credit

Rewards value, not insurance.

Pawket Care Credit is a rewards-based credit program, not insurance. Credits are earned through eligible Pet Pawket purchases and participation and may be redeemed only according to Pet Pawket's program terms.

CHARM Assistance

Charitable aid, not insurance.

CHARM Emergency Assistance is a charitable assistance program, not insurance. Assistance may be available through an application process and is subject to eligibility, mission fit, available funds, and program guidelines.

Insurance Referrals

Licensed partners handle insurance.

Pet insurance is offered by licensed insurance providers or agencies, not Pet Pawket. Coverage, eligibility, premiums, exclusions, deductibles, waiting periods, claims, and benefits are determined by the insurance provider.

## Core Copy Rules

Use warm, direct, affirming language.

Good tone:

- We honor the love families carry for their pets.
- Care has layers.
- Pet Pawket helps families plan with clarity.
- CHARM carries love forward through charitable assistance.
- Pawket Care Credit is earned rewards value.
- Licensed insurance partners handle actual insurance.

Avoid:

- Fear-based emergency pressure.
- Guilt-heavy donation language.
- Promises of coverage, reimbursement, emergency payment, policy benefits, or guaranteed assistance.
- Copy that makes Pet Pawket sound like an insurer, broker, policy seller, or claim administrator.
- Copy that blends rewards, charitable aid, and insurance into one product.

## Mandatory Disclaimers

### Short Combined Disclaimer

Not insurance. No guaranteed coverage. Pawket Care Credit is a rewards program. CHARM Emergency Assistance is charitable aid subject to eligibility, program guidelines, and available funds. Pet insurance, where available, is offered only through licensed insurance partners.

### Pawket Care Credit Disclaimer

Pawket Care Credit is a rewards-based credit program, not insurance. Credits are earned through eligible Pet Pawket purchases and participation and may be redeemed only according to Pet Pawket's program terms. Pawket Care Credit does not provide guaranteed coverage, reimbursement, or payment for veterinary expenses, emergencies, illness, injury, or any other pet-care need.

### CHARM Emergency Assistance Disclaimer

CHARM Emergency Assistance is a charitable assistance program, not insurance. Assistance may be available through an application process and is subject to eligibility, mission fit, available funds, and program guidelines. CHARM Emergency Assistance does not provide guaranteed coverage, reimbursement, or payment for veterinary expenses, emergencies, illness, injury, or any other pet-care need.

### Insurance Referral Disclaimer

Pet insurance is offered by licensed insurance providers or agencies, not Pet Pawket. Pet Pawket does not underwrite, sell, administer, or guarantee insurance policies. Coverage, eligibility, premiums, exclusions, deductibles, waiting periods, claims, and benefits are determined by the insurance provider.

## FAQ Language

### Is Pawket Care Credit Insurance?

No. Pawket Care Credit is a rewards-based credit program, not insurance. It may be earned through eligible Pet Pawket activity and redeemed only according to program terms. It does not guarantee coverage, reimbursement, or payment for veterinary expenses, emergencies, illness, injury, or any other pet-care need.

### Is CHARM Emergency Assistance Insurance?

No. CHARM Emergency Assistance is charitable aid, not insurance. Assistance may be available through an application process and is subject to eligibility, mission fit, available funds, and program guidelines. It is not guaranteed coverage or guaranteed payment.

### Can Pet Pawket Help Me Find Insurance?

Pet Pawket may offer optional referral or education paths to licensed insurance providers or agencies. Pet Pawket does not underwrite, sell, administer, or guarantee insurance policies. Coverage, eligibility, premiums, exclusions, deductibles, waiting periods, claims, and benefits are determined by the insurance provider.

### Can I Use Pawket Care Credit For Emergency Pet Expenses?

Only if future program terms allow that redemption, and only up to the available earned balance under those terms. Pawket Care Credit does not guarantee payment for emergencies, illness, injury, veterinary expenses, or any other pet-care need.

### Does Donating To CHARM Guarantee Future Help?

No. Donations and support help CHARM carry its mission forward, but they do not guarantee future assistance for any person, family, organization, or animal. CHARM Emergency Assistance depends on eligibility, mission fit, available funds, and program guidelines.

### Are Pawket Points, HeartPoints, And Care Credit The Same?

No. Pawket Points are the practical reward layer. HeartPoints are the emotional and impact layer. Pawket Care Credit is planned as rewards value that may be earned or redeemed under separate program terms if launched.

## Internal Copy Rules

### Always Separate These Lanes

- Pawket Care Credit: rewards value.
- CHARM Emergency Assistance: charitable aid.
- Insurance referrals: licensed partner path.

### Do Not Use These Words For Care Credit Or CHARM

- Coverage.
- Claim.
- Premium.
- Deductible.
- Policy.
- Covered condition.
- Underwritten.
- Reimbursement guarantee.
- Emergency protection plan.
- Guaranteed payment.

### Use These Safer Words

- Redeem.
- Request.
- Apply.
- Support.
- Assistance.
- Available balance.
- Earned rewards value.
- Charitable aid.
- Licensed partner.
- Program terms.
- Eligibility.
- Available funds.
- Mission fit.

### Story And Privacy Rules

- Do not publish real pet, rescue, adoption, emergency, medical, or memorial stories without explicit consent.
- Do not connect assistance records to Pawket Pal inspiration without separate story rights.
- Do not use Charm as a generic applicant, example emergency, sample pet, or public assistance case.
- Do not imply a public story improves eligibility for assistance, credit, or insurance.

### Account UI Rules

- Show disclaimers near balances, requests, and external partner links.
- Do not put Care Credit, CHARM Assistance, and Insurance in one undifferentiated "benefits" card.
- Do not use "claim" buttons for Care Credit or CHARM.
- Use "Redeem" for Care Credit only after terms approve a redemption path.
- Use "Apply" or "Request Assistance" for CHARM.
- Use "Visit Licensed Partner" for insurance.

### Checkout UI Rules

- Do not imply checkout creates emergency coverage.
- Do not imply a purchase guarantees CHARM assistance.
- Keep reward earning language conditional: "eligible", "may", "under program terms".
- Keep donation/impact language tied to verified campaign rules.

### Partner UI Rules

- A Pawket Network listing is not automatically a Care Credit redemption provider.
- A Pawket Verified Partner is not automatically a CHARM assistance provider.
- A CHARM-supporting partner is not automatically an insurance partner.
- Insurance partner cards require licensing and referral disclosure review.

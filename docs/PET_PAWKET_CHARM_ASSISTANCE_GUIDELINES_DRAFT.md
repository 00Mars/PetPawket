## Draft Program Identity

Working public name:

- CHARM Emergency Assistance.

Nature:

- Charitable assistance.
- Mission-guided aid.
- Subject to eligibility, mission fit, available funds, and program guidelines.
- Not insurance.
- Not Pawket Care Credit.
- Not a pet insurance referral.
- Not guaranteed support.

Safe customer framing:

> CHARM Emergency Assistance is planned as charitable aid for urgent animal-care and rescue-medicine needs. Requests may be reviewed with care, privacy, mission fit, available funds, and program guidelines in mind.

Required boundary:

> CHARM Emergency Assistance is a charitable assistance program, not insurance. Assistance may be available through an application process and is subject to eligibility, mission fit, available funds, and program guidelines. CHARM Emergency Assistance does not provide guaranteed coverage, reimbursement, or payment for veterinary expenses, emergencies, illness, injury, or any other pet-care need.

## Mission Fit

CHARM means Caring Hearts for Animal Rescue and Medicine.

The assistance path should support animal-care needs that fit CHARM's mission, especially:

- Urgent animal-care needs.
- Rescue medicine.
- Family pet-retention support.
- Shelter or rescue partner support.
- Mission-aligned animal welfare needs.

Charm is the protected memorial/origin figure at the heart of CHARM. Charm must not be used as a sample applicant, public case, placeholder, fallback story, or generic assistance example.

## Launch Position

Recommended first launch posture after review:

- Limited pilot before broad public intake.
- Manual review before automation.
- Private records by default.
- Clear request categories.
- Clear support limits.
- Clear fund-availability language.
- Separate consent for any story or public impact use.

This posture keeps the program compassionate and useful while reducing confusion with insurance, entitlement, or guaranteed payment.

## Potential Applicant Types

Possible applicant types:

- Individual pet family.
- Rescue organization.
- Shelter.
- Foster network.
- Veterinary partner referral.
- Community member requesting support for an animal-care situation.
- Internal Pet Pawket or CHARM referral.

Required decisions before launch:

- Which applicant types are allowed in the first pilot.
- Whether applicants need a Pet Pawket account.
- Whether rescue, shelter, or foster applicants need verification.
- Whether applicants must be in the United States.
- Whether repeat requests are limited.
- Whether staff or partner referrals use a separate path.

Recommended first posture:

- Start with a narrow pilot applicant set.
- Require manual review.
- Add additional applicant types only after ops capacity is proven.

Possible support categories:

- Urgent veterinary care.
- Rescue medicine.
- Emergency transport.
- Temporary boarding.
- Food or essential supplies for family pet-retention needs.
- Shelter or rescue partner support.
- Adoption-transition support.
- Other mission-aligned animal-care needs approved by CHARM.

Required decisions before launch:

- Which categories are active at launch.
- Which categories are excluded.
- What documentation may be requested.
- Whether urgent categories have faster review.
- Whether support is financial, supplies, service coordination, provider payment, partner referral, or another support type.

Recommended first posture:

- Use "support" and "assistance" language.
- Avoid public payment mechanics until charity, payments, tax/accounting, and operations review is complete.

## Potential Exclusions

Draft exclusions for review:

- Requests outside CHARM's mission.
- Requests with incomplete or unverifiable information.
- Requests where available funds cannot support the need.
- Requests that require support CHARM is not authorized or prepared to provide.
- Requests connected to fraud, abuse, resale, or unsafe activity.
- Requests that ask CHARM to act as insurance, a lender, a provider, or a policy administrator.
- Requests that require public sharing of private medical, family, rescue, adoption, or memorial details.

Exclusion copy must be reviewed carefully so it remains kind, clear, and non-accusatory.

## Request Workflow Draft

Future workflow, if approved:

1. Applicant reads program guidelines and disclaimer.
2. Applicant starts a private assistance request.
3. Applicant selects request type and support category.
4. Applicant provides only necessary context and documentation.
5. Request enters Submitted status.
6. Ops reviews for completeness, eligibility, mission fit, available funds, and privacy concerns.
7. Ops may ask for additional information.
8. Ops records outcome.
9. Applicant receives clear, kind outcome communication.
10. Any public impact/story use requires separate consent.

Do not call this a claim workflow.

## Safe Statuses

Future request statuses may include:

- Draft.
- Submitted.
- In review.
- Additional information requested.
- Approved.
- Not approved.
- Closed.
- Referred.

Avoid:

- Claim filed.
- Covered.
- Denied coverage.
- Benefit paid.
- Reimbursement pending.

## Review Factors Draft

Potential review factors:

- Mission fit.
- Urgency.
- Available funds.
- Completeness of request.
- Documentation.
- Applicant type.
- Animal welfare context.
- Rescue/shelter verification, if applicable.
- Prior request history, if applicable.
- Restricted-fund rules, if applicable.
- Privacy and safety concerns.

Review must not imply:

- Entitlement.
- Guaranteed assistance.
- Insurance-style approval.
- Guaranteed payment.
- Future eligibility.

## Documentation Draft

Possible documentation, depending on request type:

- Applicant contact information.
- Animal/pet context.
- Provider, rescue, shelter, or partner contact details.
- Estimate, invoice, care note, or support description where appropriate.
- Rescue/shelter verification where appropriate.
- Consent and privacy acknowledgments.

Data minimization rule:

- Collect only what CHARM needs to review the request.
- Avoid collecting unnecessary medical, family, financial, or hardship detail.
- Do not collect public-story consent as part of assistance approval; keep it separate.

## Support Type And Disbursement Draft

Possible support types after review:

- Direct provider support.
- Rescue/shelter partner support.
- Supply or product support.
- Transport or boarding support.
- Referral to another resource.
- Other mission-aligned support.

Required decisions:

- Whether CHARM pays providers directly.
- Whether CHARM supports applicants another way.
- Whether CHARM supports rescues/shelters through restricted campaigns.
- What payment methods are allowed.
- What documentation is required before disbursement.
- What receipts or audit records are required.

Avoid public wording that makes support sound guaranteed or insurance-like.

## Privacy And Consent Draft

Assistance records are private by default.

Separate consent is required for:

- Public CHARM impact update.
- Story card.
- Community post.
- Pawket Pal inspiration.
- Pawket Pass impact content.
- Marketing use.
- Partner update.

Public impact reporting should prefer:

- Aggregated totals.
- Consent-safe summaries.
- Non-identifying categories.
- Approved partner or campaign context.

Public impact reporting must not expose:

- Private medical details.
- Private family hardship details.
- Raw rescue, adoption, or memorial stories.
- Applicant identity without consent.
- Provider details that create safety or privacy concerns.

## Outcome Copy Draft

Tone:

- Warm.
- Clear.
- Respectful.
- Non-accusatory.
- Non-guilt-based.

Safe approval framing:

> CHARM reviewed this request and is able to offer support under the current program guidelines and available funds.

Safe not-approved framing:

> CHARM reviewed this request with care, but we are not able to offer assistance for this request under the current program guidelines and available funds.

Safe additional-information framing:

> CHARM needs a little more information before review can continue.

Do not use:

- Claim denied.
- Coverage denied.
- Benefit rejected.
- Reimbursement unavailable.

## Relationship To Pawket Care Credit

CHARM Emergency Assistance and Pawket Care Credit must remain separate.

Do not:

- Use Care Credit balance as CHARM eligibility.
- Use CHARM assistance request status as Care Credit value.
- Promise that purchases, points, donations, Pawket Pals, or HeartCodes create assistance eligibility.
- Combine assistance request status and Care Credit balance in a single "protection" panel.

## Relationship To Licensed Insurance Partners

CHARM Emergency Assistance and pet insurance referrals must remain separate.

Do not:

- Present CHARM assistance as insurance.
- Present insurance referral completion as CHARM eligibility.
- Ask CHARM reviewers to advise on insurance policies.
- Store policy details in CHARM assistance records.

## Ecosystem Connections

Potential future safe connections:

- HeartPoints for supporting CHARM campaigns, under terms.

- Pawket Pals inspired by consent-safe rescue stories after review.
- Pawket Pass impact receipts that use aggregate or approved public data.

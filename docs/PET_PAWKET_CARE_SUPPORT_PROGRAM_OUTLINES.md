## Purpose

The care-support system needs practical rules before engineering builds ledgers, requests, referrals, or account modules. These outlines capture the decisions that must be made while preserving Pet Pawket's warm tone and strict separation between:

- Pawket Care Credit as earned rewards value.
- CHARM Emergency Assistance as charitable aid.
- Licensed Insurance Partners as external insurance paths.

## Program Outline Status

Current status:

- Planning only.
- Not legally reviewed.
- Not customer terms.
- Not active program rules.
- Not eligible for implementation beyond informational copy.
- The Care Credit terms draft and CHARM assistance guidelines draft exist as internal planning artifacts only.
- The insurance partner requirements, legal review packet, and ops/support FAQ draft also exist as internal planning artifacts only.
- The implementation backlog is ticket planning only; it does not approve any ticket for build.
- Returned reviewer answers must be processed through the return-intake workbook before they change program status.
- The decision log is the approval-state source of truth; all lanes are pending review until it says otherwise.

Minimum approval before build:

- Legal review.
- Tax/accounting review.
- Privacy review.
- Payments review.
- Charity compliance review for CHARM.
- Insurance referral/licensing review for partner paths.
- Internal product and ops approval.

## Pawket Care Credit Terms Outline

### Program Identity

Working name:

- Pawket Care Credit.

Nature:

- Rewards-based credit program.
- Earned rewards value.
- Not insurance.
- Not guaranteed coverage, reimbursement, emergency payment, or veterinary payment.

Customer purpose:

- Help families carry everyday Pet Pawket participation into future eligible care paths.

### Eligibility Decisions

Define:

- Who can earn Care Credit.
- Whether guest checkout can earn later if an account is created.
- Whether accounts must be in good standing.
- Whether geographic limits apply.
- Whether business, rescue, shelter, partner, or staff accounts are eligible.
- Whether suspected fraud, abuse, refund misuse, or chargeback activity affects eligibility.

Open decision:

- Should Care Credit be available only to customer accounts at launch?

### Earn Events

Potential earn events:

- Eligible Pet Pawket purchases.
- Pawket Pack subscriptions.
- Pawket Packet purchases.
- Pawket Picks thresholds.
- Pawket Pass activity.
- Account profile completion.
- Story or journal participation, if terms allow.
- HeartPoint tier milestones, if accounting review allows.
- Heroic Quest completion, if terms allow.

Required decisions:

- Which events earn value at launch.
- Which events only earn points or badges.
- Whether donations can influence Care Credit directly.
- Whether refunds reverse earned value.
- Whether canceled subscriptions reverse earned value.
- Whether there are daily, monthly, annual, or lifetime caps.

Default safe launch posture:

- Start with a small, narrow earn set after legal/accounting approval.
- Do not connect donations, assistance, insurance referrals, or private hardship stories to Care Credit until reviewed.

### Value Rules

Define:

- How value is calculated.
- Whether value is currency, points, percentage, fixed reward, or internal credit.
- Rounding behavior.
- Minimum earn threshold.
- Maximum earn threshold.
- Expiration rules, if any.
- Terms version.
- Refund and chargeback reversals.
- Fraud hold rules.
- Whether earned value is transferable.
- Whether earned value can be cashed out.

Default safe launch posture:

- Non-cash, non-transferable, account-bound rewards value.
- Redeemable only under published Pet Pawket program terms.
- No cash-out language.

### Redemption Rules

Possible future redemption categories:

- Eligible Pet Pawket products.
- Pawket Packs.
- Pawket Packets.
- Pawket Picks or approved add-ons.
- Approved partner-service redemptions, only if contracts and legal review allow.
- Other pet-care paths only if terms and compliance review approve.

Required exclusions to consider:

- Gift cards.
- Cash equivalents.
- Donations.
- Insurance products.
- CHARM assistance requests.
- Medical or emergency expenses unless legal review explicitly approves a narrow rewards-redemption workflow.

Default safe launch posture:

- Start with Pet Pawket product redemption only.
- Do not launch veterinary, emergency, or provider redemption until a full legal and payments review is complete.

### Account Display Requirements

Future account display must include:

- Available earned value, if launched.
- Pending value, if applicable.
- Recent earn/reversal events.
- Program terms link.
- Last updated timestamp.
- Short disclaimer.

Do not show:

- Covered amount.
- Claim amount.
- Policy status.
- Emergency protection.
- Any comparison to insurance.

### Operational Requirements

Before build:

- Define ledger owner.
- Define customer support process.
- Define refund and reversal process.
- Define fraud review process.
- Define data retention.
- Define audit export needs.
- Define terms-version migration behavior.

## CHARM Emergency Assistance Guidelines Outline

### Program Identity

Working name:

- CHARM Emergency Assistance.

Nature:

- Charitable assistance.
- Mission-guided aid.
- Subject to eligibility, mission fit, available funds, and program guidelines.
- Not insurance.
- Not guaranteed coverage, payment, or reimbursement.

Customer purpose:

- Help carry love forward when urgent animal-care, rescue medicine, family pet-retention, or mission-aligned needs fit CHARM's guidelines.

### Applicant Types

Potential applicant types:

- Individual pet family.
- Rescue organization.
- Shelter.
- Foster network.
- Veterinary partner referral.
- Community member requesting support for an animal-care situation.
- Internal Pet Pawket or CHARM referral.

Required decisions:

- Which applicant types are allowed at launch.
- Whether applicants need a Pet Pawket account.
- Whether rescue/shelter applicants need verification.
- Whether geographic limits apply.
- Whether repeat requests are limited.

Default safe launch posture:

- Start with limited, manually reviewed request types and clear ops capacity.

### Support Categories

Possible support categories:

- Urgent veterinary care.
- Rescue medicine.
- Emergency transport.
- Temporary boarding.
- Food or essential supplies during a family pet-retention hardship.
- Shelter or rescue partner support.
- Adoption-transition support.
- Mission-aligned animal-care need approved by CHARM.

Required decisions:

- Which categories are eligible at launch.
- Which categories are excluded.
- What documentation is needed by category.
- Whether urgent categories have faster review.
- Whether support is direct payment, reimbursement, supplies, partner referral, or another support type.

Default safe launch posture:

- Avoid reimbursement language in public copy until payment/disbursement structure is reviewed.
- Prefer "support type" and "assistance path" language.

### Review Factors

Potential review factors:

- Mission fit.
- Urgency.
- Available funds.
- Completeness of request.
- Documentation.
- Animal welfare context.
- Applicant history.
- Partner verification.
- Privacy and safety concerns.
- Restricted-fund rules, if applicable.

Review must not imply:

- Entitlement.
- Insurance-style approval.
- Guaranteed payment.
- Guaranteed future assistance.

### Request Statuses

Safe statuses:

- Draft.
- Submitted.
- In review.
- Additional information requested.
- Approved.
- Not approved.
- Closed.
- Referred.

Avoid:

- Claim filed.
- Covered.
- Denied coverage.
- Benefit paid.
- Reimbursement pending.

### Privacy And Consent

Assistance records must be private by default.

Separate consent is required for:

- Public impact story.
- Pawket Pal inspiration.
- Community feature.
- CHARM receipt detail beyond aggregate reporting.
- Partner update.
- Marketing use.

Do not collect or publish:

- More medical detail than the program needs.
- Private family hardship details for public storytelling.
- Raw rescue, adoption, medical, or memorial stories without review.
- Charm as a sample applicant, case, or placeholder.

### Operations Requirements

Before public intake:

- Define reviewer roles.
- Define escalation rules.
- Define documentation handling.
- Define urgent-response hours or state clearly if no urgent-response window exists.
- Define decision authority.
- Define fund-source handling.
- Define disbursement process.
- Define closure reasons.
- Define applicant communication templates.
- Define appeal/reconsideration policy, if any.

Default safe launch posture:

- Build ops review before broad public intake.
- Start with limited pilot requests before open applications.

## Licensed Insurance Partner Requirements Outline

### Program Identity

Working label:

- Licensed Insurance Partners.

Nature:

- Optional education and referral lane.
- External licensed providers or agencies handle actual insurance.
- Pet Pawket does not underwrite, sell, administer, guarantee, or advise on policies unless licensing changes.

Customer purpose:

- Give families a clear path to explore actual pet insurance while keeping rewards and charitable assistance separate.

### Partner Requirements

Before a partner appears publicly:

- Confirm licensed provider or agency status.
- Confirm jurisdictions served.
- Confirm approved brand name and description.
- Confirm allowed disclosure language.
- Confirm referral compensation and affiliate disclosure requirements.
- Confirm whether outbound tracking is allowed.
- Confirm whether state-specific restrictions apply.
- Confirm partner review of Pet Pawket copy, if required.

### Public Card Requirements

Every public partner card should include:

- Partner name.
- Licensed partner framing.
- External-link cue.
- Availability note, if approved.
- Insurance referral disclaimer.
- Clear statement that policy details belong with the partner.

Do not include unless legal/licensing review approves:

- Policy recommendations.
- Premium quotes.
- Coverage comparisons.
- Deductible comparisons.
- Waiting-period advice.
- Claim outcome examples.
- "Best" or ranking labels.

### Data Handling

Default safe launch posture:

- Do not store policy details.
- Do not store application details.
- Do not store claim details.
- Do not store insurance eligibility decisions.
- Store only minimal referral metadata if tracking is approved.

Possible referral metadata after approval:

- Partner id.
- Disclosure version.
- Timestamp.
- State or jurisdiction selected.
- Outbound link id.
- Consent/acknowledgment state, if required.

## Account Dashboard Outline

The future account dashboard should show three separate modules, not one blended "protection" panel.

### Module 1: Pawket Care Credit

Empty state:

- Planned rewards value.
- No active balance yet.
- Link to Pet Care Planning.
- Short disclaimer.

Active state, only after launch:

- Available earned value.
- Pending value, if applicable.
- Recent ledger events.
- Terms link.
- Short disclaimer.

### Module 2: CHARM Emergency Assistance

Empty state:

- Planned charitable assistance path.
- No active request here yet.
- Link to guidelines when approved.
- Short disclaimer.

Active state, only after launch:

- Private request list.
- Request status.
- Next requested step, if any.
- Privacy reminder.
- Short disclaimer.

### Module 3: Licensed Insurance Partners

Empty state:

- Planned external partner path.
- No active partner referrals here yet.
- Link to Pet Care Planning.
- Insurance referral disclaimer.

Active state, only after launch:

- Approved partner cards.
- External-link cue.
- Jurisdiction note, if approved.
- Insurance referral disclaimer.

## Checkout And Commerce Outline

Checkout may eventually mention care-support only when approved.

Possible safe checkout notes:

- Eligible activity may earn rewards value under program terms.
- Some purchases may support CHARM impact where stated.
- Pet Pawket rewards and CHARM programs are separate from insurance.

Do not add checkout behavior until approved:

- Automatic Care Credit calculation.
- Care Credit redemption.
- CHARM assistance prompts tied to a user's future eligibility.
- Insurance referral prompts that look like required checkout add-ons.
- Any copy implying a purchase creates emergency support.

## Pawket Pals, Points, And Quests Outline

Safe future connections:

- Learn-about-care-planning Quest.
- Save emergency-contact checklist Quest.
- Support CHARM campaign Quest.
- Care-themed Pawket Pal accessory under program terms.
- HeartPoint progress for kindness and impact participation.
- Pawket Pack insert that explains care-support lane separation.

Do not connect:

- Pal ownership to guaranteed assistance.
- HeartPoints to guaranteed cash value.
- Private assistance records to public Pal value.
- Charm's private one-of-one Pal to any public reward.
- Insurance referral completion to emotional pressure or family worthiness.

## Decisions Needed Before Engineering

Pawket Care Credit:

- What launch earn events are allowed?
- Is redemption limited to Pet Pawket products at first?
- Are points converted to credit or are they separate?
- What are the caps, expiration rules, and reversal rules?
- Is Care Credit taxable or reportable in any scenario?

CHARM Emergency Assistance:

- Who can apply at launch?
- What request categories are eligible?
- What support types are available?
- What documentation can be requested?
- Who reviews and approves?
- How are funds tracked and disbursed?

Licensed Insurance Partners:

- Which partners are approved?
- Which states or jurisdictions are available?
- Is referral compensation involved?
- What disclosures are required?
- What tracking, if any, is allowed?

Shared:

- What public links stay visible before approval?
- What support-team FAQ is needed?
- What privacy policy updates are needed?
- What checkout copy is approved?
- What account empty states are approved?

# Pawket Admin Chart Of Accounts Baseline

Last updated: 2026-05-27

Status: baseline chart-of-accounts planning model. This is not a production accounting configuration.

## Purpose

The baseline chart of accounts gives Pawket Admin a starting vocabulary for proposed ledger records.

Accounting review must approve the production chart before real books, tax reports, investor reports, or foundation reports depend on it.

## Assets

- Cash
- Payment Processor Receivable
- Inventory
- Prepaid Expenses
- Document Vault Assets, optional/future
- Pawket Pal Digital Assets, optional/future

## Liabilities

- Sales Tax Payable
- CHARM Donation Payable
- CHERISH Donation Payable
- Pawket Care Credit Liability
- Gift Card / Store Credit Liability
- Accounts Payable
- Credit Card Payable
- Deferred Subscription Revenue

## Equity

- Owner Draws
- Retained Earnings

## Review Notes

Before production:

- Accountant must approve account names and normal balances.
- Tax treatment must be reviewed.
- CHARM and future CHERISH entity separation must be reviewed.
- Care Credit liability handling must be reviewed.
- Pawket Pal digital asset treatment must be reviewed.
- Donation income must be limited to foundation entities unless accounting/legal review says otherwise.

## Core Support Rules

Support may say:

- Care has layers.
- Pawket Care Credit is planned as earned rewards value.
- CHARM Emergency Assistance is planned as charitable aid.
- Pet insurance belongs with licensed insurance providers or agencies.
- The current Pet Care Planning page is informational only.
- Programs may require legal, compliance, privacy, payments, charity, insurance, and partner review before launch.

Support must not say:

- Pet Pawket covers expenses.
- Pet Pawket guarantees help.
- Pawket Care Credit will pay a veterinary or emergency bill.
- CHARM assistance is guaranteed.
- A purchase, donation, Pawket Pal, HeartCode, Pawket Pass, Pawket Point, or HeartPoint makes someone eligible for assistance.
- Pet Pawket sells or administers insurance.
- Pet Pawket can advise on policy terms, premiums, exclusions, deductibles, waiting periods, claims, or benefits.

## Current Not-Active State

Use this when customers ask about care-support features before launch.

### Is Pawket Care Credit active?

Safe answer:

> Pawket Care Credit is not active yet. It is planned as earned rewards value for eligible Pet Pawket activity, but no balance, earning, or redemption is available right now.

Do not say:

- "You have Care Credit."
- "Your purchase earned Care Credit."
- "Care Credit can help pay your vet bill."

### Can I apply for CHARM Emergency Assistance?

Safe answer:

> CHARM Emergency Assistance intake is not open yet. CHARM assistance is planned as charitable aid subject to eligibility, mission fit, available funds, and program guidelines after the program is reviewed and launched.

Do not say:

- "Apply now."
- "You qualify."
- "CHARM can cover this."
- "This will be reimbursed."

### Can Pet Pawket help me get pet insurance?

Safe answer:

> Pet Pawket does not offer insurance right now. In the future, Pet Pawket may provide education or links to licensed insurance partners. Actual insurance details belong with licensed providers or agencies.

Do not say:

- "We insure pets."
- "We can get you covered."
- "This policy is best."
- "Your claim will be handled by us."

### Is the Pet Care Planning page a program enrollment page?

Safe answer:

> No. The Pet Care Planning page is informational only. It explains the planned care-support lanes and how Pet Pawket keeps rewards value, charitable assistance, and licensed insurance partner referrals separate.

## General FAQ

### Is Pawket Care Credit insurance?

Safe answer:

> No. Pawket Care Credit is planned as rewards value, not insurance. It does not provide guaranteed coverage, reimbursement, or payment for veterinary expenses, emergencies, illness, injury, or any other pet-care need.

### Is CHARM Emergency Assistance insurance?

Safe answer:

> No. CHARM Emergency Assistance is charitable aid, not insurance. Assistance, if launched, would be subject to eligibility, mission fit, available funds, and program guidelines.

### Are Pawket Care Credit and CHARM the same thing?

Safe answer:

> No. Pawket Care Credit is planned as earned rewards value. CHARM Emergency Assistance is planned as charitable aid. They are separate paths with separate rules.

### Are Pawket Points, HeartPoints, and Care Credit the same?

Safe answer:

> No. Pawket Points are the practical reward layer, HeartPoints are the emotional and impact layer, and Pawket Care Credit is planned as a separate rewards-value program if launched.

### Does donating to CHARM guarantee future help?

Safe answer:

> No. Donations and support help CHARM carry its mission forward, but they do not guarantee future assistance for any person, family, organization, or animal.

### Does buying from Pet Pawket guarantee CHARM assistance?

Safe answer:

> No. Purchases may support Pet Pawket and future CHARM impact programs where stated, but no purchase guarantees charitable assistance.

### Does owning a Pawket Pal affect Care Credit or CHARM assistance?

Safe answer:

> Not right now. Pawket Pals may later connect to safe badges, quests, or education, but Pal ownership does not guarantee rewards value, assistance, or insurance.

### Does a HeartCode store assistance or insurance details?

Safe answer:

> HeartCodes should not expose private medical, assistance, hardship, or insurance information. They are intended for consent-safe identity, provenance, sharing, and future ecosystem connections.

## Future Launched-State Guardrails

Only use these after a lane launches and approved terms/guidelines exist.

### If Care Credit launches

Support may say:

- Available balance, if shown in the account, is earned rewards value under program terms.
- Pending value may depend on purchase eligibility, refund windows, or review rules.
- Redemption is limited to approved categories under program terms.

Support must not say:

- Care Credit guarantees payment for pet-care needs.
- Care Credit replaces insurance.
- Care Credit is CHARM assistance.
- Care Credit can be cashed out unless terms explicitly allow it.

### If CHARM assistance intake launches

Support may say:

- A request can be submitted through the approved intake path.
- Requests are reviewed for eligibility, mission fit, available funds, and program guidelines.
- Additional information may be requested.
- Assistance records are private unless separate consent allows public story use.

Support must not say:

- A request will be approved.
- CHARM can pay before review.
- A request is a claim.
- A not-approved outcome means coverage was denied.

### If insurance partner referrals launch

Support may say:

- Pet Pawket may link to licensed insurance partners.
- The partner handles insurance details.
- Questions about coverage, eligibility, premiums, exclusions, deductibles, waiting periods, claims, and benefits should go to the licensed partner.

Support must not say:

- Pet Pawket recommends a specific policy.
- Pet Pawket can answer policy-detail questions.
- Pet Pawket can file or manage a claim.
- Partner availability means a customer or pet is eligible.

## Escalation Triggers

Escalate to legal/compliance or program leadership if a customer asks:

- Whether Pet Pawket can pay a specific bill.
- Whether Care Credit can be used for a specific veterinary or emergency expense.
- Whether a CHARM request will be approved.
- Whether a donation creates future eligibility.
- Whether a policy covers a condition or treatment.
- Whether Pet Pawket recommends one insurance provider over another.
- Whether private story, medical, rescue, adoption, memorial, or hardship details can be shared publicly.

Escalate to privacy/security if a customer provides:

- Medical records.
- Financial hardship details.
- Family hardship details.
- Rescue or adoption records.
- Insurance policy details.
- Sensitive personal information not requested by an approved workflow.

## Sample Support Responses

### Customer: I have an emergency. Can Pet Pawket cover my vet bill?

Safe response:

> I am sorry this is heavy right now. Pet Pawket does not provide guaranteed coverage or emergency payment. Pawket Care Credit and CHARM Emergency Assistance are not active programs yet, and neither is insurance. If CHARM assistance opens in the future, requests would be reviewed under program guidelines, mission fit, and available funds.

### Customer: I bought something. Did I earn Care Credit?

Safe response:

> Pawket Care Credit is not active yet, so purchases are not earning Care Credit right now. The current Pet Care Planning page is informational while program terms are being developed and reviewed.

### Customer: I donated to CHARM. Can I get help later?

Safe response:

> Thank you for helping carry the mission forward. Donations do not guarantee future assistance. If CHARM assistance opens in the future, requests would be reviewed under eligibility, mission fit, available funds, and program guidelines.

### Customer: Which pet insurance should I choose?

Safe response:

> Pet Pawket cannot recommend a policy or advise on insurance details. If licensed insurance partner referrals are added later, policy questions will need to be handled by the licensed provider or agency.

### Customer: Can my pet's story become a Pawket Pal if CHARM helps us?

Safe response:

> Assistance and story use are separate. Private assistance details stay private by default. Any public story, Pawket Pal inspiration, or community use would require a separate consent and review process.

## Internal Notes For Future Agents

- Keep support language warm, but do not soften the legal boundary into a promise.
- Preserve Charm as protected memorial/origin content; never use Charm as a support example.
- Do not create sample assistance cases using real or implied private hardship.
- Do not combine Care Credit, CHARM, and insurance into one "protection" answer.
- If uncertain, route to the care-support docs and keep the answer informational.

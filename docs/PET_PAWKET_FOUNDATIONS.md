# Pet Pawket Foundations

Last updated: 2026-05-15

This document preserves the foundation-level identity for CHARM, CHERISH, Charm, and how those missions relate to Pet Pawket. Use it with `docs/PET_PAWKET_CANON.md`, `docs/PET_PAWKET_PROJECT_OVERVIEW.md`, and `docs/PET_PAWKET_CARE_SUPPORT_ECOSYSTEM.md`.

## CHARM Foundation

Full name:

> Caring Hearts for Animal Rescue and Medicine

CHARM is the animal rescue and medicine foundation connected to Pet Pawket. Its mission is animal-focused, rescue-oriented, medicine-oriented, practical, hopeful, and grounded in love.

CHARM can support:

- Animal rescue.
- Urgent medicine.
- Emergency animal care.
- Rescue partner needs.
- Shelter support.
- Family pet-retention support during hardship.
- Adoption and new-beginning campaigns.
- Charm's Day annual rescue, memory, and love-forward event.

CHARM may eventually receive donations, round-ups, a portion of eligible sales, campaign proceeds, special-edition support, or community-event proceeds, but every public claim about donation handling, restricted funds, receipts, assistance, and impact needs review.

## Charm

Charm is the emotional heart of Pet Pawket's mission lineage and the heart of CHARM.

Charm was Jonas's beloved dog, rescued in Tyler, Texas. Her life should be framed as long, beautiful, loved, intelligent, loyal, gentle, joyful, and victorious. Charm's story is not a tragedy hook. It is an origin story about love carried forward.

Charm must never be used as:

- A generic demo pet.
- A seed pet.
- A placeholder.
- A fallback name.
- A sample account pet.
- A mock-data pet.
- A generic memorial example.
- A public reward.
- A public collectible.
- An ordinary rescue case.
- A commercial product character.

Use neutral alternatives such as "your pet", "a pet profile", "sample pet", "protected origin story", or "a consented story" in examples and tests.

## Charm's Private Pawket Pal

Charm's Pawket Pal is a private one-of-one tribute.

It may be referenced internally as a protected origin tribute, but it must never become a public collectible, randomized reward, marketplace item, drop, product insert, public Community Pal, or user-claimable Pal.

This rule protects the dignity of Charm's role and keeps the Pawket Pal system from commercializing the private source of the mission.

## CHARM Emergency Assistance

CHARM Emergency Assistance is a planned charitable aid lane, not insurance.

It may later support urgent veterinary care, rescue medicine, emergency animal care, hardship-based family pet-retention support, or rescue partner needs, but only after guidelines, entity/compliance review, ops capacity, privacy controls, data boundaries, and disbursement rules are approved.

Required boundary:

> CHARM Emergency Assistance is a charitable assistance program, not insurance. Assistance may be available through an application process and is subject to eligibility, mission fit, available funds, and program guidelines. CHARM Emergency Assistance does not provide guaranteed coverage, reimbursement, or payment for veterinary expenses, emergencies, illness, injury, or any other pet-care need.

Do not imply:

- Guaranteed help.
- Guaranteed payment.
- Guaranteed eligibility.
- Claims, coverage, policy benefits, premiums, deductibles, or reimbursements.
- That purchases or donations make a specific person, family, rescue, or animal eligible for future assistance.
- That requesting or receiving help grants public story rights.

## CHERISH Foundation

Full name:

> Caring Hearts Enabling Resilience and Inspiring Happiness

CHERISH is a future sibling mission, not the same as CHARM.

CHARM is animal rescue and medicine. CHERISH is the later human, family, youth, resilience, happiness, and community-uplift companion mission.

CHERISH may eventually use a similar structure to Pet Pawket and CHARM, but it should remain conceptually related and operationally separate until a dedicated scope exists.

Do not:

- Collapse CHERISH into CHARM.
- Use CHERISH to justify animal-assistance workflows.
- Use CHARM docs as direct CHERISH approval.
- Build CHERISH public surfaces in this repo unless explicitly requested.
- Mix CHERISH/youth or family hardship data into Pet Pawket, Pawket Pal, HeartCode, or CHARM records without legal/privacy review.

## Shared Foundation Tone

Foundation copy should be:

- Warm.
- Hopeful.
- Practical.
- Dignified.
- Love-forward.
- Clear about what is active and what is planned.
- Protective of private stories and sensitive details.

Avoid:

- Guilt-driven fundraising.
- Shock imagery.
- Exploitative grief.
- Urgent pressure that makes a family feel judged.
- Unsupported impact claims.
- Public medical, rescue, adoption, assistance, or memorial details without consent and review.

The foundation layer should make people feel that love can keep moving, not that they are being pressured into proving it.

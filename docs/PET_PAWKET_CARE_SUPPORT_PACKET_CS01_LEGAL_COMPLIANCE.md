## Lane Separation To Review

Pawket Care Credit:

- Earned rewards value.
- Not insurance.
- Not charitable assistance.
- Not guaranteed veterinary or emergency payment.

CHARM Emergency Assistance:

- Charitable aid.
- Subject to eligibility, mission fit, program guidelines, available funds, and privacy controls.
- Not insurance.
- Not guaranteed aid.

Licensed Insurance Partners:

- External licensed-provider paths only.
- Pet Pawket must not act as insurer, underwriter, seller, broker, administrator, policy advisor, comparison advisor, or claims handler unless properly licensed and appointed in a later reviewed model.
